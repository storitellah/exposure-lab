### Photographic scenes

Scenes may also ship photographic layers in `public/scenes/<scene-id>/`.
These are AI-generated images (prompts in `scripts/photo-scenes.json`), not
photographs of real people or places, prepared with
`scripts/prepare-photo-scene.sh`.

**Source and terms.** The images are generated with Higgsfield
(higgsfield.ai) on Storitellah's account, under Higgsfield's Terms of Use for
the free plan. Higgsfield's help centre ("Who owns my generations and can I
use them commercially?") states that:

- Higgsfield does not claim ownership of outputs (Terms of Use, section 4.4);
- the Terms of Use do not restrict commercial use of outputs, and commercial
  use is not tied to a particular plan;
- plans that add watermarks embed them at generation time;
- outputs may not be used to train, fine-tune or improve AI models.

Some third-party summaries describe the free plan as personal-use only;
Higgsfield's own help centre is followed here. The Terms of Use are the
binding text: re-check https://higgsfield.ai before each release, and do not
ship an image that carries a Higgsfield watermark (regenerate it, or keep the
drawn scene) — watermarks must not be cropped or edited out.

The images show no real, identifiable people. They are distributed with
the app on the same basis as the rest of its artwork; reuse outside the app
is subject to Higgsfield's terms.
